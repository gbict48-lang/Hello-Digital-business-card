Privacy Policy

Hello Digital Business Card · Last updated 2026

Hello Digital Business Card (“the app”) is made by GB ICT. We designed it to respect your privacy.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. There are no analytics or tracking SDKs.

Where your data lives

The cards you create — names, contact details, photos and company logos — are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to us.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

If you choose to sign in, Apple provides your name and email so we can personalise your first card. This is stored only on your device and is never shared. You can use the entire app without signing in.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is handled entirely by Apple via the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Apple Wallet

Passes you create are generated and signed on your device and added to Apple Wallet by you. We do not receive them.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Because your data stays on your device, you remain in full control — delete a card, or delete the app, and it is gone.

Questions? Email hello@gbict.nl.
